## Short description

Read-only IMP Agent beside every Classic Acumatica screen. Existing account permissions apply.

## Detailed description

Ask IMP gives authorized International Media Products employees a conversational
assistant alongside their Classic Acumatica workspace.

Click the Ask IMP button on imp-ttr.acumatica.com or the extension toolbar icon to
open a browser side panel. Sign in with your Acumatica account and select a persona
authorized for your account. If only one persona is available, it opens automatically.

Depending on your account permissions, ask about IMP prices, recent sales prices,
inventory availability, sales orders and shipping, production orders, IMP product
logs, or administrative usage reports. Use # to find an item and * to find a customer.
Answers can include readable tables, source references and links to Acumatica orders.

Chat tools are read-only. Make order, shipping and production changes in Acumatica
or IMPUI. Authorized administrative screens retain their own access controls.

An authorized IMP account or configured Acumatica onboarding is required. Installing
this extension does not grant access to customer information or additional personas.
Sales customer access remains restricted by the backend's assignment checks.

The extension embeds the existing hosted IMP Agent interface. It does not read
Acumatica form fields, collect browsing history, or copy Acumatica cookies. It adds
one launcher button on the configured ERP host. The hosted service processes the
questions you submit and retrieves authorized information to answer them.

This extension is for International Media Products users and is not an official
Acumatica product. AI responses should be checked against the linked source records.

## Distribution

Choose Unlisted in the developer dashboard. An unlisted link can be forwarded;
the link is not an access-control mechanism. Backend sign-in and authorization
remain mandatory. Google review is still required.
